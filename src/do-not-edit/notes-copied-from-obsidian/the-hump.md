* India China Wing (ICW) under AF10 -8/1/44→ India China Division (ICD) of ATC
	* ICD also controlled XX Bomber Command's Second Mobile Transport Squadron
* AF10 provided protection to the ICD in the air and Air Service Command (ASC) provided ground supplies.


Stilwell's farewell words:
> The Air Transport Command needs no commendation from anybody. It has accomplished a tremendous task under great difficulties. It has flown unarmed planes over enemy territory in fair weather and foul, and set up a record for delivering the goods that is unequalled anywhere.
